Those black streaks on your roof aren't dirt. They're a cyanobacteria called *Gloeocapsa magma* that feeds on the limestone filler in asphalt shingles. Left alone, it spreads, holds moisture, and shortens the life of the roof.

Our roof cleaning uses **no pressure**. A roof-safe solution is applied gently and left to work, killing the algae without disturbing the granules that protect your shingles. Results are visible within hours, and the roof keeps clearing over the following weeks as rain carries away the dead growth.
