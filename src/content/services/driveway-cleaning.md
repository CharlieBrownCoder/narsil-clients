Your driveway is the first thing people see. It's also the surface that takes the most abuse: tires, oil, leaves, and whatever the weather brings.

We clean concrete with a **rotary surface cleaner**, not a wand. A wand leaves stripes. A surface cleaner spins two high-pressure jets under a hood, cleaning in even passes and leaving a uniform finish. Afterward, a post-treatment brightens the surface and slows the return of mildew.

## Surfaces we clean

- Driveways and aprons
- Sidewalks and front walks
- Patios and pool decks
- Garage floors
- Curbs and retaining walls

## Add concrete sealing

Ask about sealing after cleaning. A penetrating sealer repels water and oil, keeps the clean look longer, and reduces freeze-thaw damage.
