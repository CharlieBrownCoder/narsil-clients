Green streaks on the north side of the house. Black spots creeping up the siding. A driveway that used to be light gray. In our climate, it takes about a year for algae and mildew to take hold, and once they do, they spread.

Our house washing service removes all of it. We use a **soft wash** method: low pressure, a biodegradable cleaning solution, and a thorough rinse. It's the method siding manufacturers recommend, and it keeps surfaces clean two to three times longer than pressure alone because it kills the growth rather than just blasting off the top layer.

## What's included

- Full exterior wash of siding, brick, stucco, or stone
- Soffits, fascia, and gutters' exterior faces
- Window exteriors rinsed (spot-free rinse available)
- Entry doors, garage doors, and trim
- Landscaping pre-wet and post-rinsed

## Surfaces we clean

Vinyl, brick, painted wood, Hardie board, stucco, stone veneer, and aluminum. If you have a surface not listed here, send a photo and we'll tell you how we'd approach it.
