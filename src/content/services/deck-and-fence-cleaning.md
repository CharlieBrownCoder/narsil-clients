Wood turns gray because UV light breaks down the surface fibers. Add a season of rain and you get mildew in the grain. The fix isn't more pressure, it's the right chemistry.

We clean decks and fences with a **wood-safe cleaner** at low pressure, then apply a brightener that restores the original color. If you want the result to last, we'll come back once it's dry and apply a penetrating stain.
