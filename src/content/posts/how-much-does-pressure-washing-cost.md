Pricing is the first thing everyone asks, so here's a straight answer. These are typical ranges we see across Plano, Frisco, McKinney, and Allen.

| Service | Typical range | What changes the price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| House washing (single story) | $199 to $350 | Square footage, siding type, mildew severity |
| House washing (two story) | $300 to $550 | Height, access, number of gables |
| Driveway cleaning | $149 to $300 | Length, oil stains, sealing |
| Roof cleaning | $349 to $800 | Pitch, square footage, moss |
| Deck or fence cleaning | $199 to $600 | Linear feet, staining |

## What makes a job cost more

1. **Size.** A 4,000 square foot two-story takes twice as long as a 1,800 square foot ranch.
2. **Condition.** Heavy algae, years of buildup, or oil stains need pre-treatment and extra passes.
3. **Access.** Tight lots, steep roofs, and pool enclosures slow things down.
4. **Add-ons.** Sealing, gutter cleaning, or window rinsing are usually worth bundling.

## How to get the best price

Bundle services. A house wash plus driveway is almost always cheaper together than on two separate trips, because the setup cost is shared. Ask about it when you request a quote.
