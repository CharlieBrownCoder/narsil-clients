From the historic square to Craig Ranch, McKinney homes range from century-old painted wood to brand-new brick and stone. We've worked on all of it.

McKinney's mature trees are beautiful, but they keep roofs and north walls shaded, which is exactly where algae and moss thrive. If your home sits under oaks or pecans, plan on an exterior wash every year or so.
