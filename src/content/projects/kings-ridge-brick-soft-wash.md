The homeowners called after an HOA notice about algae on the north side of their two-story brick home in Kings Ridge. The growth had been building for about three years and had spread onto the painted trim and soffits.

## What we found

Brick and mortar on the north elevation were covered in a green film, with black mildew streaks under every window sill. The painted trim was chalky, which ruled out any pressure above a garden-hose level.

## What we did

We pre-wet the landscaping, then applied a soft-wash solution to the full exterior from the ground with a telescoping wand. Dwell time was about fifteen minutes. A low-pressure rinse removed everything, and we spot-treated the sills a second time. The whole job took two technicians just under three hours.

## Result

The brick came back to its original red, the trim rinsed clean without any paint loss, and the HOA closed the notice the following week. We recommended a wash every eighteen months given the tree cover on that side.
